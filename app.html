SharpZone

What is in focus, and where to focus so everything from near to far is sharp.

About this

Standard thin-lens model: hyperfocal H = f² / (N × c) + f, with near and far limits from H and the focus distance. The circle of confusion c is a convention for acceptable sharpness on a normal-size print, not a physical law; pixel peepers should use a smaller one. Checked against the Canon Europe 28 mm f/16 example (1.63 m before adding f) and the lensandshutter.com 50 mm f/8 at 10 ft example (about 6 ft 3 in total). Real lenses focus a little differently, so leave a safety margin. About
